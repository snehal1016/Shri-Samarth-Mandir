THE SAINT

Shri Samarth Ramdas Swami

A Life of Devotion, Discipline & Purpose.

Shri Samarth Ramdas Swami was a revered saint, spiritual teacher and poet of Maharashtra. His birth name was Narayan, and Jamb Samarth is remembered as the place of his birth.

His spiritual tradition places strong emphasis on devotion to Lord Rama and Hanuman, discipline, self-control and purposeful living. His teachings and writings continue to form an important part of the Samarth tradition.

THE MANDIR

A Place of Remembrance.

Preserving the Memory of Samarth.

The Shri Samarth Mandir at Jamb was established in memory of Samarth Ramdas Swami and stands as an important place of devotion connected with his life and legacy.

Jamb is also home to the historic Ram Temple associated with the Samarth family. Together, these places form an important part of the religious and cultural heritage of Jamb Samarth.

JAMB SAMARTH

THE BEGINNING

Born at Jamb

Born as Narayan, the younger son of Suryajipant Thosar and Ranubai, Samarth Ramdas Swami was born at Jamb on Ram Navami in 1608. Jamb became the foundation of the spiritual journey that would shape his life and teachings.
